Help

Installing

Mac: how do I install it?
  1. Download the version for your Mac — Apple silicon (M1–M4) or Intel — and open the DMG.
  2. Drag Live Music into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app — click Done.
  4. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Live Music. You only need to do this once.
Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

That appears for new apps Windows hasn't seen much yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. You'll only need to do this once.

Android: how do I install the APK?
  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back and tap Install.
  3. To update later, download the new version and install it over the top. Your library stays.
Will my library carry over?

Yes. Sign in with the same account and your loved songs, playlists and history sync to every device.
