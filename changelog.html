22.1.0

28/10/2026

New

  • Devices. See what your other devices are playing and control them — play, pause, skip, seek and volume — or move playback across with Play on and Play here. Works between your own Mac, PC and phone.
  • Smarter search. The top result can be a song, album or artist — whatever fits best.
  • Music videos on artist pages.
  • Continue watching. Reopen the app and pick up the last video where you left off.
  • Get the other apps from your account menu, plus a link to this website.

Changed

  • Friend cards show album art and are easier to read.
  • Long podcasts and videos show hours (1:39:45 instead of 99:45).
  • The "Resume music" button now sits apart from the video.
  • Android: the Sign in / Create account switch is easier to tell apart from the main button.

Fixed

  • Videos didn't play in the downloadable Mac and Windows apps — they now stream in full quality.
  • The message box in friend chats could be hidden behind the player bar.
  • Notifications that sometimes didn't appear.

22.0.0

26/10/2026

Live Music now comes to Windows and Android, with a new home for downloads.

New

  • Live Music for Mac and Windows, no Glaze needed. Download the app directly — a native Mac app (Apple silicon and Intel) and a native Windows app.
  • New welcome and sign-in screens on every platform, with clearer sign-in and create-account forms and password reset.
  • Messages. Chat with friends right in the Friends tab, and send them a song in one tap.
  • Visualiser. A live visualiser on its own screen, or glowing behind the big Now Playing view in the colours of the album art. Adjust its strength in Settings.
  • Weather. Tap the weather on Home to expand it for more detail, using your approximate or exact location.
  • Picture-in-picture. Keep a video playing in a floating window you can move and resize, even outside Live Music.
  • Video quality. Choose the quality of videos, and full screen now fills the screen and shows the video's title.
  • Right-click any song for quick options, including Add to Queue.
  • Rearrange your queue by dragging songs into the order you want.
  • Apple Music and TIDAL. See and control what's playing in those apps, just like Spotify (Mac and Windows).
  • Logged-in devices. See every device signed in to your account and log out any of them.
  • Permissions page in Settings, showing what Live Music can access and why.

Changed

  • Everything is free. The Free, Pro and Ultra plans have ended for now. Everyone gets every feature.
  • Dark theme is now true black.
  • Cleaner menus throughout, sized to fit in with everything around them.
  • Android: Spotify has been removed from the phone app. Songs from Spotify in your library play from YouTube instead.

Fixed

  • Videos no longer claim to be 1080p when they aren't.
  • Songs could play silently on Windows.
  • The app could close with "Something went wrong" when a synced song came from an unrecognised service.
